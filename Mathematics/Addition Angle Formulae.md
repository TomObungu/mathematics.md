It can be proven that:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\sin(A + B) = \sin A\cos B + \cos A\sin B \\ \\
\cos(A + B) = \cos A\cos B + \sin A\cos B \\ \\ 
\tan(A + B ) = \frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}
\end{gather*}
$$
 It can be proven the same for subtraction:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\sin(A - B) = \sin A\cos B - \cos A\sin B \\  \\
\cos(A - B ) = \cos A \cos B + \sin A\sin B \\ \\
\tan(A - B) =  \frac{\tan A - \tan B}{1+\tan A\tan B}
\end{gather*}
$$
