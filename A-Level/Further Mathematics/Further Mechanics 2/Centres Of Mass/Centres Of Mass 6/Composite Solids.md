Like with lamina and frameworks, we can use standard results, as well as these obtained from integration to find the centre of mass of a composite body.

# Example 1
Uniform solid formed of a cylinder and a cone. Find the distance of the centre of mass from O. 
![[Pasted image 20260219002512.png]]

Like how we could use area with a uniform lamina, we can use volume for a composite body.

Set up the table in the same way:

| Object            | Cylinder               | Cone                   | Total                   |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Volume            | $\frac{3}{2}\pi a^{3}$ | $\frac{1}{3}\pi a^{3}$ | $\frac{11}{6}\pi a^{3}$ |
| Dist. From O, $x$ | $\frac{3}{4}a$         | $\frac{7}{4}a$         | $\bar{y}$               |
Setting up the formula:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{3}{2}\pi a^{3}\left( \frac{3}{4}a \right) + \frac{1}{3}\pi a^{3}\left( \frac{7}{4}a \right) = \frac{11}{6}\pi a^{3}\bar{y} \\ \\ 
\frac{41}{24}a = \frac{11}{6}\bar{y} \\ \\
\bar{y} = \frac{41}{44}a
\end{gather*}
$$

# Example 2
Uniform solid made from a solid cylinder, radius $\frac{3}{2}a$, height $2a$.

A hemisphere, radius, a is drilled out, centre O, centred on top of the cylinder. Find the distance of the CoM from O.
![[Pasted image 20260219003012.png]]

Setting up the table again:

| Object       | Cylinder               | Hemisphere              | Total                   |
| ------------ | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Volume       | $\frac{9}{2}\pi a^{3}$ | $-\frac{2}{3}\pi a^{3}$ | $\frac{23}{6}\pi a^{3}$ |
| Dist. From O | a                      | $\frac{3}{8}a$          | $\bar{y}$               |
Setting up the formula:

$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{9}{2}\pi a^{3}(a) - \frac{2}{3}\pi a^{3}\left( \frac{3}{8}a \right) = \frac{23}{6}\pi a^{3}\bar{y} \\ \\ \\
\frac{17}{4}a = \frac{23}{6}\bar{y} \\ \\
\therefore \bar{y} = \frac{46}{51}a \text{ From O}
\end{gather*}
$$
