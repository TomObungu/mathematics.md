You may have to find the centre of mass of a solid made from component solids of different densities. We apply the same approach as with laminas - scaling the volumes by their densities. 

# Example 1 
A key is made from a solid plastic hemisphere, radius $\frac{3}{2}a$, density $\rho$, and a metal cylinder, radius $\frac{1}{2}a$, height $3a$, density $3\rho$. 

The hemisphere and cylinder are centred on the same axis. Find the distance of the centre of mass from O.

| ----------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----- |
| Volume      | $\frac{9}{4}\pi a^{3}$ | $3\left( \frac{3a}{2} \right)$ |       |
