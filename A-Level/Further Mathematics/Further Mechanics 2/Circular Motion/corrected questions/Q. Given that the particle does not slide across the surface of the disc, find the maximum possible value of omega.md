In this problem, you must realise that the centripetal force is a combination of tension and friction. You must also use the equation for tension, $T =\frac{\lambda x}{l}$, as mentioned in further mechanics 1. 

Drawing a diagram of the particle from the top view:
![[Pasted image 20260224123905.png]]

From the diagram we know that $T+F_{r}$ is the centripetal force, so we can equate it to the centripetal force formula. We are given $\omega$, so we use $F = m\omega^{2}r$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
F = T + F_{r} \\ \\
m\omega^{2}r = \frac{\lambda x}{l} + \mu mg \\ \\
4(\omega^{2})(2) = \frac{(12)(0.5)}{1.5} + 0.3(4)(9.8)
\end{gather*}
$$
We can the rearrange for $\omega$:
$$
\omega = \sqrt{ \frac{\frac{12(0.5)}{1.5} + 0.3(4)(9.8)}{4(2)} } = \frac{\sqrt{ 197 }}{10}
$$
Therefore:
$$
\omega \leq \frac{\sqrt{ 197 }}{10}
$$
