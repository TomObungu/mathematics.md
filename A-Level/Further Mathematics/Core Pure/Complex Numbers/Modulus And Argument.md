Since a complex number, $z$ can be represented as a vector in the complex plane, we describe its value by its $|z|$ and the its argument $arg(z)$.
![[Pasted image 20251111233559.png]]
The argument is usually specified in the range $-\pi<arg(z)\leq\pi$, called the 'principle argument':
![[Pasted image 20251111233916.png]]
The argument can also be given in the range $0\leq arg(z)<2\pi$. In this case $arg(z)$ will be given anticlockwise form the $\mathrm{Re}$ axis:
![[Pasted image 20251111234332.png]]

# Worked Example 3
Given that $arg(\lambda-9i+z)=\frac{\pi}{4}$, find $\lambda$:
![[Pasted image 20251111234737.png]]
We know that the angle between the $\mathrm{Im}$ and $\mathrm{Re}$ from the complex number will be the $arg(z)$, therefore $\tan\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$ will be equal to the $arg(z)$:

As the vector forms a triangle with the planes we can see that:
$$
\tan\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{4}{\lambda+10}
$$
This forms an equation we can solve for $\lambda$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\lambda+10 = \frac{4}{\tan\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)} \\
\lambda = -6
\end{gather*}
$$

$$
