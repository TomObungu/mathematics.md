A quadratic equation of the form $ax^{2} +bx+c=0$, where $a,b,c$  are integers, can have roots $\alpha$ and $\beta$. 

You can determine the relationship between the coefficients of the terms in the quadratic equation and the values of $\alpha$ and $\beta$

If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are roots of the equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ then:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a} \\ \\ 
\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}
\end{gather*}
$$
