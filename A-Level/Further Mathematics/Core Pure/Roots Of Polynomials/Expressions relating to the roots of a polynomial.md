# Rule for reciprocal sums
Quadratic:
$$
\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} = \frac{\alpha+\beta}{\alpha\beta}
$$
Cubic:
$$
\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} + \frac{1}{\gamma} = \frac{\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma +\beta\gamma }{\alpha\beta\gamma}
$$
Quartic:
$$
\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} + \frac{1}{\gamma} + \frac{1}{\delta} = \frac{\alpha\beta\gamma + \alpha \beta\delta + \alpha\gamma\delta + \beta\gamma\delta}{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}
$$

# Rule for product powers
The product of the roots raised to the power is equal to the individual roots raised to the power multiplied by each other.

# Sum Of Squares

# Sum Of Cubes
The sum of cubes have their specific cases. 
## Quadratic 
The sum of the cube of the roots is the sum of the roots cubed subtract $3\alpha\beta(a+\beta)$
$$
\alpha^{3} + \beta^{3} = (\alpha+\beta)^{3} - 3\alpha\beta(\alpha +\beta)
$$
## Cubic
The sum of cube of the roots is the sum of roots cubed subtract 3 multiplied by $\left( \sum \alpha \right)\left( \sum\alpha\beta \right)$ plus $\alpha\beta\gamma$
$$
\alpha^{3} + \beta^{3} + \gamma^{3} = (\alpha+\beta+\gamma)^{3} - 3(\alpha + \beta + \gamma)(\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma + \beta\gamma) + 3\alpha\beta\gamma
$$
