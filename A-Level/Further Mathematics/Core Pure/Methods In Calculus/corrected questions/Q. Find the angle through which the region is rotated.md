This is a standard volumes of revolution problem, however you must realise that it is not a full revolution around the $x$ axis. Due to that, we will be integrating sectors with respect to $\frac{1}{2}\theta(fx)^{2}$. $$
\therefore V = \frac{\theta}{2}\int_{8}^{\frac{1}{8}}\left( 2x^{\frac{1}{3}} + x^{\frac{-2}{3}} \right)dx 
$$
