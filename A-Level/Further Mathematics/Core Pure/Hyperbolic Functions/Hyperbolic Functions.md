From Euler's identity, we know that:
$$
e^{ix}=\cos x + i\sin x
$$
and:
$$
e^{-ix} = \cos x - i\sin x
$$
Thus it possible to use these equations to form identities for $\cos x$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
e^{ix}+e^{-ix} = 2\cos x \\ \\
\frac{e^{ix}+e^{-ix}}{2} = \cos x \\ \\
\end{gather*}
$$
 and $i\sin x$:
 $$
 \begin{gather*}
e^{ix}-e^{-ix} = 2i\sin x \\ \\
\frac{e^{ix}-e^{-ix}}{2} = i\sin x
\end{gather*}
$$
