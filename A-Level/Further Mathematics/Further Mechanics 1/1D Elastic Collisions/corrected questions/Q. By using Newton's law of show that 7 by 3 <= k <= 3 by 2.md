Secondly, form equations of momentum and rearrange for $v_{1}$
a
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Conservation of momentum} : \\
mu = mv_{1} + km\left( \frac{3}{10}u \right) \\
u = v_{1} + \frac{3}{10}uk \\ \\ 
\implies v_{1} = u - \frac{3}{10}uk\\
\implies v_{1} = u\left( 1-\frac{3}{10}k \right)
\end{gather*}
$$
b For part b, the question is asking for the limits of k such that $e$ will lie between 0 and 1. Therefore as the question indicates, you need to substitute the value of $e$ in terms of $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ when $e=0$ and when $e=1$
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Newton's law of restitution} : \\ 
v_{2} - v_{1} = e \\ \\
\frac{3}{10}u - u - \frac{3}{10}uk = e\\
\frac{3}{10}k - \frac{7}{10} = e \\ \\
\text{When } e = 0: 
\frac{3}{10}k - \frac{7}{10} = 0 \implies k=\frac{7}{3} \\ \\
\text{When } e = 1: 
\frac{3}{10}k - \frac{7}{10} = 1 \implies k=\frac{17}{3}
\end{gather*}
$$
Therefore:
$$
\frac{7}{3} \leq k \leq \frac{17}{3}
$$
