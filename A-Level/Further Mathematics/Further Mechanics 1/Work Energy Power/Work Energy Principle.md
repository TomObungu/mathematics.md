Due to the conservation of energy - The energy within a closed system, therefore the energy within the universe is constant.

In questions, since we are dealing with changes over a field length of time, we can write this as:
$$
\text{Energy (at the start) = Energy (at the end)}
$$
The types of energy that will fit into the Energy (at the start) category are:
- GPE
- KE
- $E_{k}$ (Elastic potential energy)
- Working Force (Work)

The types of energy that will fit into the Energy (at the end) category are:
- The given types of energy at the end of passage of time
- Work done against friction and other resistive forces e.g. drag, friction

# Work
It possible to define the external Working force (Work) that is not done by friction as: 
$$
W=Fs [Nm][J]
$$
Where $F$ is the external force and $s$ is the distanced moved done with the working force. 


# Work against friction 
It possible to work out the work done against friction as:
$$
W_{F}=F_{r}s
$$
Recall that $F_{r}$ requires $\mu$ and $R$ where:
$$
F_{r}=\mu R
$$
This work done against friction will be energy dissipated in the form of heat and will end up in the Energy(at the end) part of the equation.

# GPE (Gravitational Potential Energy)
GPE is the work that gravity would do on the particle if it were to fall h meters. By possessing GPE, the object has the potential do work.

The height h, and the GPE is relative to where we define our zero point to be.  This means it possible to chose where the zero point - the point where GPE = 0 to be where is convenient.

# Worked Example 3
A 5kg particle moves along a horizontal surface adjacent to a resistive force, F. It has initial velocity 20$ms^-1$. After 15m, its velocity is $8ms^-1$. Find F.
$$
\begin{align*}
&E_{\text{start}} &= &E_{\text{end}} \\ 
&KE_{\text{start}} &= &W_{F} + &KE_{\text{end}} \\
&\frac{1}{2}(5)(20)^{2} &= &15F +&\frac{1}{2}(5)(8)^{2} \\
&15F&=&840\\
\therefore &F&=&56
\end{align*}
$$
