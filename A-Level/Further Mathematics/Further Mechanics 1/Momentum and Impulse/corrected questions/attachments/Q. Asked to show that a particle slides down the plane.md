For such questions, remember that a particle will slip down a plane if the maximum frictional force opposing the force is less than force downwards:

1. Calculate $F_{max}$:
$$
\cos\alpha = \frac{4}{5} \qquad \mu=\frac{1}{4} \qquad R = \left( \frac{4}{5}mg \right) \implies F_{max} = \frac{1}{4}\left( \frac{4}{5}mg \right) = \frac{1}{5}mg
$$
2. Compare $F_{max}$ with force opposing the friction down the slope:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Force down the slope}: mg\sin\alpha = \frac{3}{5}mg \\ \\
\frac{3}{5}mg > \frac{1}{5}mg > F_{max} \\
\therefore \text{The box will immediatley slide down the plane}
\end{gather*}
$$
