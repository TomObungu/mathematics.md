In general:
$$
\int f(ax+b) = \frac{1}{a}F(ax+b)
$$
That is $F(x)$ is the integral result of the function.
E.g $\int \cos(2x+3)$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
ax+b=(2x+3) \qquad f(x) = \cos(ax+b)  \qquad F(x) = \left( \frac{1}{a} \right)\sin (ax+b)
\\ \\ \int \cos(2x+3) = \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x+3)
\end{gather*}

$$
E.g. $\frac{1}{3x+2}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
ax+b = 3x+2 \qquad f(x)=\frac{1}{(ax+b)} \qquad F(x)=\frac{1}{a}\ln(x+b)\\ \\
\int \frac{1}{3x+2} = \frac{1}{3}\ln(3x+2)
\end{gather*}
$$
E.g $(2x+3)^{4}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
ax+b = 2x+3 \qquad f(ax+b) = (ax+b)^{4} \qquad F(x) = \left( \frac{1}{a} \right)\frac{1}{5}(ax+b)^{5} \\ \\
\int (2x+3)^{4} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{10} \right)(2x+3)^{5} = \frac{1}{10}(2x+3)^{5}
\end{gather*}
$$
E.g. $3e^{2x-1}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
ax+b = 2x-1 \qquad f(x) = e^{ax+b} \qquad F(x) = \left( \frac{1}{a} \right)e^{ax+b} \\ \\
\int 3xe^{2x-1} = \frac{3}{2}e^{2x-1}
\end{gather*}
$$
