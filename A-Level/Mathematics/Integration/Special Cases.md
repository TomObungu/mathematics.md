## $\int x^{2}e^{x^{3}}$
The case of  $\int x^{2}e^{x^{3}}$ is a special case as it can be evaluated entirely using revers chain rule. Being able to do this can also simplify doing a similar integration by parts problem. 

Do not be haste to try and do integration by parts but instead consider $y=e^{x^{^{3}}}$ . Do not try $y=e^{n+1}$ but $y=e^{n}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
y = e^{x^{3}} \\ \\
\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^{2}e^{x^{3}}
\end{gather*}
$$
Therefore:
$$
\int x^{2}e^{x^{3}} = \frac{1}{3}e^{x^{3}}
$$
