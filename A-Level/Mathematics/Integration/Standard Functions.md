## Integrals of standard functions:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\int x^{n} = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} \\ \\
\int e^{x} = e^{x} + c \\ \\
\int \frac{1}{x} = \ln|x| + c \\ \\
\int \cos x = \sin x \\ \\
\int \sin x = -\cos x \\ \\
\int \sec ^{2} x = \tan x \\ \\
\int \csc \cot x = -\csc x \\ \\
\int \csc ^{2} x = -\cot x \\ \\
\int \sec x \tan x = \sec x 
\end{gather*}
$$
## Integrals for exponential and logarithms:
The integral of any function $a^{x}$
$$
\int a^{x} = \frac{1}{\ln a}a^{x}
$$
The integral of any function $\log_{a}x$:
$$
\int \log_{a}x=\frac{x}{\ln a}(\ln x-1)
$$
