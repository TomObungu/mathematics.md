With this problem in particular, it is really tempting to to by parts. By parts will work however, it much faster to approach it using the reverse chain rule.
![[Pasted image 20251201155703.png]]
For the cases when you have:
$$
\begin{gather*}
e^{x^{n}}
\end{gather*}
$$
Then do not try $y=e^{n+1}$ but $y=e^{n}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
y = e^{x^{3}} \\ \\
\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^{2}e^{x^{3}}
\end{gather*}
$$
Therefore:
$$
\int x^{2}e^{x^{3}} = \frac{1}{3}e^{x^{3}}
$$
