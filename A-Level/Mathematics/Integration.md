## Standard functions
Integrals of standard functions:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\int x^{n} = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} \\ \\
\int e^{x} = e^{x} + c \\ \\
\int \frac{1}{x} = \ln|x| + c \\ \\
\int \cos x = \sin x \\ \\
\int \sin x = -\cos x \\ \\
\int \sec ^{2} x = \tan x \\ \\
\int \csc \cot x = -\csc x \\ \\
\int \csc ^{2} x = -\cot x \\ \\
\int \sec x \tan x = \sec x 
\end{gather*}
$$
More standard functions:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\int \tan x = \ln|\sec x| \\ \\
\int \sec x = \ln|\sec x + \tan x| \\ \\
\int \cot x = \ln|\sin x| \\ \\
\int \csc x = \ln|\csc x -\cot x|
\end{gather*}
$$
## Integrating $f(ax+b)$
In general:
$$
\int f(ax+b) = \frac{1}{a}F(ax+b)
$$
That is $F(x)$ is the integral result of the function
E.g $\int \cos(2x+3)$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
f(x) = \cos(x)  \qquad F(x) = \sin x
\\ \\ \int \cos(2x+3) = \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x+3)
\end{gather*}
