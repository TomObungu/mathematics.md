We know that the function is a cubic function and it has a real root given. In order for a triangle to be formed, the function must have complex conjugate roots. This is one possible arrangement of the real root and the complex conjugate pair:
![[Pasted image 20251122210508.png]]
We know that the conjugate pair will in the form $z_{2}=(x+iy)$ and $z_{3}=(x-iy)$.  As well as that from the diagram we know that base of the triangle will have length:
$$
y - -y = 2y
$$
and the height of the triangle will be:
$$
3-x
$$
From this we can form an equation for the area of the triangle:
$$
\begin{gather*}
A_{\text{triangle}} = \frac{bh}{2} \\ \\
= \frac{2y(3-x)}{2} \\ \\
= y(3-x) = 35
\end{gather*}
$$
As well as that we can form another equation using the roots in the form $x\pm iy$ and $(z-3)$ as the linear term in factorised form:
$$
(z^{2}-2xz+x^{2}+y^{2})(z-3) = 0
