For such as question that involves evaluating the sum with a modified limit e.g.:
![[Pasted image 20250927125534.png]]
You can see that limit is divided by 2 in order to evaluated. However this may pose a problem for odd values of n as odd values of n will yield a non-integer value of n which may cause the evaluation not to work. e.g.:
![[Pasted image 20250927125647.png]]
becomes:
$$
\sum_{r=1}^{50}r((-1)^{r}+2r)^{2} - \sum_{r=1}^{13}r((-1)^{r}+2r)^{2}
$$
The second part of the expression cannot be evaluated as for $2n=13$, $n=\frac{13}{2}, n \notin \mathbb{N}$
