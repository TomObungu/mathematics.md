For such questions it is better to evaluate the entire expression first rather than the expression inside the brackets. 

It is also handy to use

$$
(\sum_{r=1}^nr)^{2} = \sum_{r=1}^nr^{3}
$$

Evaluate:

$$
\begin{gather*}
\sum_{r=1}^n(1+2r)^{3} \\ \\
=\sum_{r=1}^n(8r^{3}+12r^{2}+6r+1) \\ \\
= 8\left( \frac{10(11)}{2} \right)^{2} + 12\left( \frac{1}{6}(10)(11)(21) \right) +6\left( \frac{10(11)}{2} \right) + 10 \\ \\
= 21960

\end{gather*}
$$
