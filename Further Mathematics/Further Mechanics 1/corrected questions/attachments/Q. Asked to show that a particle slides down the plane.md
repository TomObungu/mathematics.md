For such questions, remember that a particle will slip down a plane if the maximum frictional force opposing the force is less than force downwards:

1. Calculate $F_{max}$:
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{4}{5} \qquad \mu=\frac{1}{4} \qquad R = \left( \frac{4}{5}mg \right) \implies F_{max} = \frac{1}{4}\left( \frac{4}{5}mg \right) = \frac{1}{5}mg
$$
