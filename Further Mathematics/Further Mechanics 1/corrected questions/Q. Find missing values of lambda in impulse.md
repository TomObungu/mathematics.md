With this information it is possible to set up an equal for the magnitude in terms of $\lambda$. To set up the equation for $\lambda$ . Express the equation for impulse in terms of $v$ and $\lambda$ and rearrange for $v$ :$$
\begin{gather*}
\lambda(-i+j)=\frac{1}{2}(v - 8i) \\ \\
-2\lambda i + 2i = v - 8i \\ 
(8-2\lambda)i + 2\lambda j = v
\end{gather*}
$$
Once an equation for v has been written we can then use Pythagoras and equate to the magnitude:
$$
\begin{gather*}
(8-2\lambda)^{2}+(2\lambda)^{2} = (4\sqrt{ 10 })^{2} \\ 
8\lambda^{2} - 32\lambda - 96 = 0 \\
\cancel{ \lambda = -2  }\qquad \lambda = 6
\end{gather*}
