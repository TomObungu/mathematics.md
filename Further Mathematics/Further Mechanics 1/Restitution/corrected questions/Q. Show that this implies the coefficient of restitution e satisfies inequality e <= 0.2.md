To set things up, form equations from conservation of momentum and Newton's law of restitution:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Converstation of momentum } : \\ \\
m(2u) - m(3u) = mv_{1} + mv_{2} \\ 
-u = v_{1}+v_{2} \qquad (1) \\ \\
\text{Newton's law of restituion} : \\ \\
\frac{v_{2} - v_{1}}{5u} = e \\
v_{2} - v_{1} = 5eu \qquad (2)\\
\end{gather*}
$$
If you know that motion is reversed for both particles, then both $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ are acting in opposite directions and will have have their signs flipped. If $u_{2}$ was negative then $v_{2}>0$. Therefore to prove this we need to find an equation for $v_{2}$ in terms of $e$ and $u$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
(1) + (2): \\ 
2v_{2} = 5eu-u \\
2v_{2} = u(5e-1)\\
v_{2} = \frac{u}{2}\left( 5e-1 \right)
\end{gather*}
$$
As $v_{2} > 0$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{u}{2}(5e-1) > 0 \\
5e-1 > 0 \\
5e>1 \\
e > \frac{1}{5} \\
QED
\end{gather*}
$$
