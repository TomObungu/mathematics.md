If you were stretch a string by a small value $dx$. Since $dx\to_{0}$, The force $F$ will be equal to $T$ as T is constant over this distance.
![[Pasted image 20251017211621.png

$$
F=T=\frac{\lambda x}{l}
$$
By streching a string over with a force $F$ over a distance $dx$. We do work $Fdx$.  If we stretch the string from $x_{0}$ to $x_{1}$  
![[Pasted image 20251017211953.png]]
The total work will be:
$$
\int_{x_{0}}^{x1} Fdx = \int_{x_{0}}^{x1} \frac{\lambda x}{l}dx = \left[ \frac{\lambda x^{2}}{2l} \right]_{x_{0}}^{x_{1}}
$$
This work has been transferred into elastic potential energy:
$$
EPE=\frac{\lambda x^{2}}{\text{2l}}
$$
With EPE we can now use it to solve WEP questions with springs and strings:
# Worked Example 1

$$
\begin{gather*}
F = 0.2R = 0.2(2g) = 0.4g \\ \\

\frac{20(0.5)^{2}}{2} = \frac{1}{2}(2)v^{2} + 0.5(0.4g) \\

\boxed{v=0.735}

$$
