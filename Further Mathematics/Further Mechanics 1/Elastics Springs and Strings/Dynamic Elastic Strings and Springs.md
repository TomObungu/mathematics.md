# Worked Example 1
A string has a natural length 0.5m and modulus of elasticity $25N$.
A 1kg particle is attached at $B$ and held at a position $0.75m$ below $A$. Calculate it's initial acceleration
![[Pasted image 20251017173635.png]]
This question involves equating the forces in the system using $F=ma$ however you must use the equations for $T$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
T = \frac{25(0.25)}{0.5}=12.5 \\
T - g = a \\ 
12.5 - g = a \\
\therefore a = 2.7ms ^{-1}
\end{gather*}
$$
