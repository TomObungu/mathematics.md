(a) For this question you need to realise the mass hanging in equilibrium will exert a force downwards and the string **must** have tension to counteract it. If there is tension then there must be an extension of the string and you must consider it when finding $OE$:
Diagram:
![[Pasted image 20251108110421.png]]
$$
\begin{gather*}
T - mg = 0 \\ \\
\frac{(4mg)(x)}{2a} - mg = 0 \\ \\
\frac{2x}{a} = 1 \\ \\
x = \frac{1}{2}a
\end{gather*}

$$

Therefore the length $OE$ is: $\frac{5}{2}a$
