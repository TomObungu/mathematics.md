## Why?
Due to the string being extended downwards towards A, past its natural length at B, the tension in the upper segment of the strings naturally points upwards. This causes the particle particle to also pull up at the lower segment of the spring. If the lower segment is being pulled upwards the lower segment of the string exerts a Tension force downwards.

Secondly you must realise that extensions and thereby tensions  in the lower and upper segments of the string are different.
