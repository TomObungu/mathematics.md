Conservation Of Momentum:
$$
\begin{gather*}
m(u)+em(eu) = mv_{1} + emv_{2} \\ 
u + e^{2}u = v_{1} + ev_{2} \\ \\
\end{gather*}
$$
Newton's Law Of Restitution:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{v_{2}-v_{1}}{u-eu} = e \\ 
v_{2} - v_{1} = eu -e^{2}u
\end{gather*}
$$
Speed of Q = $v_{2}$, Finding $v_{2}$: 
$$
\begin{gather*}
v_{1} + ev_{2} = u+e^{2}u \\ 
v_{2} - v_{1} = eu-e^{2}u \\ \\
ev_{2} + v_{2} = eu+u \\ 
v_{2}(e+1) = u(e+1) \\ \\
v_{2} = u \\ 
QED
\end{gather*}
$$
For this question you need to show that $v_{1}$ is greater than 0.

It is always the case that if the coefficient is in terms of $e$ and a quadratic, complete the square and show that the quadratic is greater than 0

Otherwise if it is a linear combination of $e$ then form an inequality of the coefficient being $>0$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Speed of P} = v_{1} \\ 
u-v_{1} = eu-e^{2}u \\ \\
e^{2}u-eu+u = v_{1} \\
u(e^{2}-e+1) = v_{1} \\ \\
u\left( \left( e-\frac{1}{2} \right)^{2}+\frac{3}{4} \right) = v_{1}
\end{gather*}
$$
$$
\begin{gather*}
\left( e-\frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4} > 0 \\ \\
\therefore \text{Speed Of P} > 0 \\ 
QED
\end{gather*}
$$
