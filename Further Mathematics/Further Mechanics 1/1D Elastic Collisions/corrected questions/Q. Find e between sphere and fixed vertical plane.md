# First collision
Diagram:
![[Pasted image 20251108162521.png]]
Conservation Of Momentum:
$$
\begin{gather*}
m(-u) = 8m(v_{1}) + m(v_{2}) \\ \\
-u = 8v_{1} + v_{2} \\ 
\end{gather*}
$$
Newton's Law Of Restitution:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{v_{2}-v_{1}}{u} = \frac{7}{8}\\ \\
v_{2}-v_{1} = \frac{7}{8}u \\
\\
8v_{2}-8v_{1} = 7u
\end{gather*}
$$
Finding $v_{2}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
8v_{1}+v_{2} = u \\ 
8v_{2}-8v_{1} = 7u \\ \\
9v_{2} = 6u \\
v_{2} = \frac{2}{3}u
\end{gather*}
$$
Finding $v_{1}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{2}{3}u-v_{1} = \frac{7}{8}u \\
\frac{2}{3}u -\frac{7}{8}u = v_{1} \\ \\
v_{1} = -\frac{5}{24}
\end{gather*}
$$
