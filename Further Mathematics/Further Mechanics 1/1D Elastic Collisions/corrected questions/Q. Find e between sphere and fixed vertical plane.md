Conservation Of Momentum:
$$
\begin{gather*}
m(-u) = 8m(v_{1}) + m(v_{2}) \\ \\
-u = 8v_{1} + v_{2} \\ 
\end{gather*}
$$
Newton's Law Of Restitution:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{v_{2}-v_{1}}{u} = \frac{7}{8}\\ \\
v_{2}-v_{1} = \frac{7}{8}u \\
\\
8v_{2}-8v_{1} = 7u
\end{gather*}
$$
