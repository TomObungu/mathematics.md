Conservation Of Momentum:
$$
\begin{gather*}
m(kV) + \lambda m(V) = \lambda mv_{2} \\ 
kV+V = \lambda v_{2} \\
V(k+\lambda) = \lambda v_{2} \\ \\
v_{2} = \frac{V(k+\lambda)}{\lambda}
\end{gather*}
$$
Newton's Law Of Restitution:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{v_{2} - 0}{kV-V} = e \\ \\
v_{2} = eV(k-1) \\ \\
\frac{V(k+\lambda)}{\lambda} = eV(k-1) \\ \\
e=\frac{V(k+\lambda)}{\lambda V(k-1)} \\ \\
= \frac{k+\lambda}{\lambda(k-1)} \\
QED
\end{gather*}
$$
