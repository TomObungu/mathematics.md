(a) For these type of questions, the best approach is to draw a diagram and form equations from the principle of conservation of momentum and Newton's law of restitution. Usually the speed of Q will end being $v_{1}$. Always remember to denote any unknown values of $v$ to the right:
 ![[Pasted image 20251020092352.png]]
 $$
 \begin{gather*}
\text{Momentum Before} = \text{Momentum After} \\
3m(2u)-4m(u) = 3m(v_{1})+4m(v_{2}) \\
6u - 4u =3v_{1} + 4v_{2} \\
2u = 3v_{1} + 4v_{2}
\end{gather*}
$$
