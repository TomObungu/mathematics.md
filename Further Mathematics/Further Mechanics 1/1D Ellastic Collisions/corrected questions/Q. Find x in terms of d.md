If the direction of P is not changed as a result of the collision, then $v_{1}$ is positive. We need to find $v_{1}$ in terms of $u$ and $e$.
$$
\begin{gather*}
8v_{1} = u - 15eu \\ \\
v_{1} = \frac{u}{8}(1-15e) \\ \\
v_{1}>0 \\
\frac{u}{8}(1-15e) > 0 \\
1-15e > 0 \\ 
1>15e \\ 
\frac{1}{15} > e \\ \\
\text{Since the particles can coalesce even when motion of P is not changed } \\ 
\implies e \geq 0: \\  \\
\therefore \boxed{0\leq e<\frac{1}{15}}
\end{gather*}
$$
