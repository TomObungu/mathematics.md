When the particles collide for the second time, they are  a distance $x$ from the wall. The particles collide a second at time $T$.

Given that $e=\frac{1}{18}$. We can calculate $v_{1}$,$v_{2}$ and $v_{3}$,
$$
v_{1} = \frac{u}{48} \qquad v_{2} = \frac{3u}{16} \qquad v_{3} = -\frac{1}{3}\left( \frac{3u}{16} \right) = -\frac{u}{16}
$$
For particle P, the particle moves a distance $d-x$. From $s=\frac{d}{t}$. We know that $T = \frac{d}{s}$. From then we can work out the value of $T$ for P:
$$
\begin{gather*}
T = \frac{d-x}{v_{1}} \\ \\
= \frac{d-x}{\frac{u}{48}} \\
= \frac{48(d-x)}{u}
\end{gather*}
$$
Knowing the same for $T$. We can calculate the T. We know that it will first take $t_{1}$ to reach the wall and $t_{2}$ again to travel distance $x$. This combined time of $t_{1}+t_{2}$ will also equal to $T$. 
$$
\begin{gather*}
t_{1} = \frac{d}{v_{2}} = \frac{d}{\frac{3u}{16}} = \frac{16d}{3u} \\ \\
t_{2} = \frac{x}{|v_{3}|} = \frac{x}{\frac{u}{16}} = \frac{16x}{u} \\ \\
T= t_{1}+t_{2} = \frac{16d}{3u} + \frac{16x}{u}
\end{gather*}
$$
We have two separate equations for $T$, therefore we can now equate them an form an equation for $x$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{48(d-x)}{u} = \frac{16d}{3u} + \frac{16x}{u} \\ \\
3(d-x) = \frac{d}{3}+x \\ \\
9d - 9x = d + 3x \\ \\
12x = 8d \\ \\
x = \frac{2}{3}d
\end{gather*}
$$
