In this question you must realise that we are not given the directions of travel after the impact because we are given the speeds, so we must account for each case of direction to find the two values of $k$.
 Diagrammatically, the two cases of direction are:
 ![[Pasted image 20251015201844.png]]
 (a) Using the equation for impulse:
 $$
 \begin{gather*}
\frac{3}{2} mu = m(2v-u) \\
\frac{1}{2}u =2v \\
v = \frac{u}{4}
\end{gather*}
$$
(b) Case when A moves to the right:
$$
\begin{gather*}
3m(ku) - m(u) = 3m(v) + m(2v) \\ 
3ku-u = 5v \\
3ku-u = \frac{5}{4}u \\
3ku = \frac{9}{4}u \\
k = \frac{3}{4}
\end{gather*}
$$
Case when A moves to left:
$$
\begin{gather*}
3m(ku) - m(u) = -3m(v) + m(2v) \\
3ku - u = -v \\
3ku - u = -\frac{u}{4} \\
3ku = \frac{3}{4}u \\
k = \frac{1}{4}
\end{gather*}
$$
Therefore:
$$
k = \frac{3}{4} \qquad k = \frac{1}{4}
$$
