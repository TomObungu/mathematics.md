For questions like this when given the impulse in terms of a vector and the kinetic energy you must always take the velocity as $v=i^{2}+j^{2}$:

Firstly we need to write I in terms of $\lambda$ using $I=m(v-u)$when velocity $u = (-i+2j)$ and rearrange for $v$ to later be used in the kinetic energy equation:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\lambda(2i-j) = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)(v - (-i+2j)) \\ \\
v = 2\lambda(2i-j)+(-i+2j) \\ \\
v = (4\lambda-1)i+(-2\lambda+2)j
\end{gather*}
$$
