If the question involves some form of trigonometric proof:
1. Label the parallel and perpendicular components of each collision
2. Form equations in terms of $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$ for the components
	1. If angles are given always write them in exact form early on in workings if possible
3. Find missing velocities by squaring the sin and cosine of the components e.g. $(1)^{2}+(2)^{2}$
4. Find the tangent of the angles e.g. $\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\tan\theta$
5. Consider the impulse of the restitution only in the perpendicular component
6. If given half angle formula e.g. $\frac{1}{2}\alpha$ and asked to compare it with full angle $\alpha$, then remember double angle formula and write it in terms of $\frac{1}{2}\alpha$
	1. When the angle is written in double angle form, try substituting $t=\frac{1}{2}\alpha$ and solve for t.
	2. Once $t$ is found, solve for $\frac{1}{2}\alpha$
7. If asked to find a specific value of a trigonometric form, try think of identities such as $\tan ^{2}\theta +1 = \sec ^{2}\theta$
If the questions involves using an inequality:
8. Look at any slopes and check if their gradient is lower than the incoming collision vector and compare the restitution
9. If asked to prove an inequality, try and find the $\tan$ of the angles that are needed in inequality and compare
10. Check if any angles are simply $\theta-90$ which in most cases lead to the angle being $\cos\theta / \sin\theta$ or or $\cot\theta$
	1. Check if any angles are $\cos(360-\theta)=\cos\theta$, $\sin(180-\theta)$, $\tan(90+\theta)=\tan\theta$, otherwise the angles are the negative version of $\cos,\sin$ or $\tan$
If the question involves kinetic energy:
11. Always try find the missing velocities by squaring the $\sin$ and $\cos$ of components then substitute them into $\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}$
If the question does not involve vectors bu the angles are not perpendicular:
12. Try and find the value of any missing angles that are opposite each other in the collision vector and use their values in calculations for any missing speeds
