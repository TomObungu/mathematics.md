 (a)
 Starting off with the diagram: ![[Pasted image 20251103081152.png]]
 Writing out the parallel and perpendicular components:
 $$
\begin{gather*}
v_{1}\cos\beta = v\cos\theta\\ 
v_{1}\sin\beta = \frac{1}{3}v\sin\theta \\ \\
v_{2}\cos\gamma = v_{1}\sin\beta \\ 
v_{2} \sin \gamma = ev_{1}\cos\beta
\end{gather*}
$$
Using the fact that the question states that half of its kinetic energy is lost, we can calculate $v_{1}$ to use in an equation for KE:
$$
\begin{gather*}
v_{1}^{2} = (v\cos\theta)^{2} + \left( \frac{1}{3}v\sin\theta \right)^{2}  \\ \\
v_{1}^{2} = v^{2}\cos ^{2}\theta +\frac{1}{9}v^{2}\sin ^{2}\theta
\end{gather*}
$$Forming an equation for $v$ and $v_{1}$ in terms of KE:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac{1}{2}mv_{1}^{2} = \frac{1}{2}mv^{2} \\ \\
\frac{1}{2}m\left(v^{2}\cos ^{2}\theta +\frac{1}{9}v^{2}\sin ^{2}\theta\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}\right] \\ \\ 
\cos ^{2}\theta + \frac{1}{9}\sin ^{2}\theta = \frac{1}{2} \\ 
\cos ^{2}\theta +\frac{1}{9}(1-\cos ^{2}\theta) = \frac{1}{2} \\
9\cos ^{2}\theta -\cos\theta +1 = \frac{9}{2} \\ 
8\cos ^{2}\theta = \frac{7}{2} \\
\cos ^{2}\theta = \frac{7}{16} \\ \\
\boxed{\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{ 7 }}{4}}
\end{gather*}
$$

Working out $v_{2}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}

v_{2}^{2} = \left( \frac{1}{4}v \right)^{2} + \left( \frac{\sqrt{ 7 }}{4}ev \right)^{2} \\ v_{2}^{2} = \frac{1}{16}v^{2} + 7
